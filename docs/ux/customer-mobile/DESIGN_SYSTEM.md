# Design System – Tirodhan Customer Mobile

## Brand tone
- Calm
- Sacred
- Respectful
- Minimal
- Modern Indian
- Trustworthy and clear

## Colors
### Primary gold
`#C58A3A`

Use for:
- primary CTA fills,
- active tab indicators,
- key icon accents,
- selected controls,
- subtle dividers and ornaments.

### Primary text
`#111111`

### Cream background
Recommended implementation base: `#F8F5EF`

### Supporting neutrals
- Secondary text: `#6B6B6B`
- Border: `#E8DDCC`
- Soft gold tint: `#E4D2BC`
- Error: `#C64545`

## Typography
### Brand wordmark
Use a serif family such as:
- Cormorant Garamond
- Playfair Display

### Major display headings
For large headings such as:
- Activity
- Account
- Review your collection
- Give sacred items a respectful onward journey

Use the approved serif/display treatment.

### App UI text
Use **Inter** for:
- body text,
- labels,
- form text,
- buttons,
- supporting descriptions,
- tab labels,
- metadata.

Suggested weights: 400 / 500 / 600.

### Hindi / Devanagari
Preferred:
- Tiro Devanagari Hindi

Fallback:
- Noto Serif Devanagari

Hindi must be live text in the app.

## Shape language
- Rounded cards
- Soft or nearly-flat shadows
- Rounded fields and selectors
- Circular quick-category frames on Home
- Center-emphasized circular Book Pickup tab action

## Layout
- Warm cream breathing room
- Dense enough to feel like a serious consumer app
- Clear vertical rhythm
- Distinct section separation

## Iconography
- Simple, modern icons
- Gold accent where relevant
- Curated collection imagery for category cards
- Avoid playful/cartoonish iconography
