# Screen Spec – Collection Home

## Purpose
Main discovery and entry surface for sacred collection.

## Approved structure
1. Brand header
2. Current/selected address strip
3. Notification/help icons
4. Hero card:
   - “Sacred Collection”
   - large hero headline
   - supporting copy
   - “Book a pickup” CTA
   - devotional imagery
5. Hero pager indicator
6. Quick circular category strip
7. Grouped horizontally scrollable sub-category sections:
   - Floral & Organic
   - Sacred Objects
   - Texts & Paper
   - Vastra & Accessories
8. Your Activity summary
9. Based on your previous collections
10. Bottom navigation

## Critical rule
Do **not** flatten category content into a single grid.

The approved structure is:
- quick circular top-level categories,
- followed by multiple grouped horizontal pagers.

## Tone
Category labels should describe respectful handover/disposal, not purchase intent.
