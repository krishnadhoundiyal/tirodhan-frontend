# Customer Mobile UX Review v1

**Status:** Awaiting product UX approval

**Scope:** Customer Mobile — app launch/authentication, Home, Activity, Account, Sacred Collection entry and early collection screens

## Review board

![Tirodhan Customer Mobile UX Review](./customer-mobile-ux-review-v1.jpg)

## What is being reviewed

This board establishes the first concrete Customer Mobile screen direction before implementation begins.

The review focuses on:

- visual hierarchy and overall look/feel;
- authentication screen treatment;
- Home as the Tirodhan service-discovery surface;
- Activity and Account presentation;
- Sacred Collection entry and early collection journey screens;
- clarity, usability and appropriateness of the customer-facing copy;
- consistency with modern native mobile conventions.

## Product constraints

These constraints are already agreed and should not be redesigned away during visual review:

1. **Tirodhan is the application; Sacred Collection is one service.**
2. Customer Mobile must remain capable of adding future independent services such as:
   - idol mending/restoration collection;
   - recycled/reused idol commerce;
   - Rent-a-Cow.
3. Future services must not be forced into the CollectionRequest model.
4. Standard mobile UI/functionality should use established platform patterns rather than invented interaction models.
5. Backend behaviour remains authoritative for actual flows, payloads, states and permissions.
6. This review is for screen UX/visual direction, not for redefining backend architecture.

## Current visual direction

- calm, respectful and contemporary;
- warm without becoming ornamental or temple-app themed;
- restrained use of religious imagery;
- strong whitespace and clear hierarchy;
- conventional native controls and navigation;
- service imagery/content provides product character rather than unusual controls.
